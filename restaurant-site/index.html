Abang Chor has stood at the same wok on Jalan Besar for nineteen years. Flat noodles, cockles from the morning market, chives, a duck egg if you ask — one plate per fry, because two plates in the wok means no wok hei. The stall opens at six and closes when the noodles are gone.

Good to know

  • No pork and no lard — everything is fried in vegetable oil.
  • Cockles go into the kuey teow by default. Say tanpa kerang and we leave them out.
  • The vegetarian plate is cooked in a separate wok, but the same stall handles seafood.
  • Tables are shared on a busy night. Bungkus if you are in a hurry.
